Youth Sports Access

No young person should miss an opportunity to participate because their family cannot afford it.

PATH Foundation aims to reduce financial barriers so more young people can take part in local sports, travel teams, and the experiences that help them grow.

Get HelpSupport Youth Sports

Participation costs add up

For many families, the expense goes far beyond a registration fee. Young athletes may need equipment, transportation, accommodations, or an accompanying adult to participate safely.

Gear and entry fees

Uniforms, shoes, equipment, registration, tournament entry fees, and sports or enrichment camp fees.

Travel and family support

Transportation, hotel stays and necessary travel expenses for parents, guardians or other responsible adults accompanying youth.

Camps and safe places to practice

Potential assistance with sports camps, skill-building clinics, practice facility rental, and related insurance expenses.

More opportunities. Fewer barriers.

From grade school through college, PATH hopes to help young people in Native and underserved communities stay involved, build confidence, make connections, and keep moving toward their goals.
