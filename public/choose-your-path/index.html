Our Mission

PATH means Providing Access to Hope. Our goal is to help young people see possibilities for their futures through visits to colleges, technical schools, military installations and academies, along with team-building experiences. We also aim to help youth sports teams with uniforms, shoes, lodging and travel costs.

What We Do

Explore Future Careers

Planned visits to colleges, trade and technical schools, military bases and academies, with opportunities to experience new places and build teamwork.

Support Youth Sports

Fundraising goals include uniforms, athletic shoes, team travel, lodging and related expenses.

Community Fundraisers

Support specific youth projects and follow progress toward each fundraising goal.
