Our Mission

PATH means Providing Access to Hope. Our goal is to help young people see possibilities for their futures through visits to colleges, technical schools, military installations and academies, along with experiences that build confidence, encourage social connections, and introduce new possibilities. We also aim to help youth sports teams with uniforms, shoes, lodging and travel costs.

What We Do

Explore Future Careers

Planned visits to colleges, trade and technical schools, military bases and academies, with opportunities to explore new places, meet people, develop confidence, and broaden their horizons. Experiences like the SEMA Show are among the visits we hope to organize.

Support Youth Sports

Fundraising goals include uniforms, athletic shoes, team travel, lodging and related expenses.

Current Fundraising Goals

Help Bring NFHS Network Streaming to Frontier School

Our goal: Help Frontier Elementary & Middle School explore and fund an NFHS Network-compatible sports camera and any required setup so home games can be shared with families near and far.

This is not just for Frontier families. When visiting schools come to Frontier to compete, their parents, grandparents, relatives, and supporters could also watch their students play—even when travel, work, distance, or other responsibilities keep them from attending.

The NFHS Network uses automated Pixellot cameras that follow the action and can make school-approved games available live and on demand through its streaming service. Viewers may need an NFHS Network subscription. Availability and installation at a K–8 school like Frontier must be confirmed with NFHS.

Building Bridges at Every Stage

From grade school and middle school through high school and college, PATH Foundation aims to help young people overcome barriers and keep moving forward. We focus especially on young people in Native and underserved communities, where limited resources can make the next step feel out of reach.

Through encouragement, mentorship, exposure to educational and career opportunities, and practical support, we hope to strengthen connection, confidence, and a sense of possibility. Our long-term goal is to help reduce risk factors associated with substance misuse, alcohol misuse, and suicide by giving youth support, hope, and meaningful opportunities. These are goals, not claims of proven outcomes.
